This site is deliberately plain: one column, system-light JavaScript, and type doing most of the work.

It's built with [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/), a single-binary static site generator. Articles are Markdown; the layout is a handful of Tera templates.

The typeface is **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** from the Braille Institute, chosen for its clarity and generous letterforms, with **Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono** for code. Body text runs in a blue you've seen a thousand times, with a warm red-orange reserved for accents.

Light and dark themes follow your system preference and can be toggled; the choice is remembered. Hosted on a CDN, rebuilt automatically on every merge.

The mark is a **PM monogram** — Peter Miller — drawn as a single brush gesture: the P in the theme's ink color and the M in the accent, set just left of the name and enlarged on the home page. It's hand-built SVG, so it stays crisp at any size and is reused as the favicon, which adapts to your browser's light or dark setting.
