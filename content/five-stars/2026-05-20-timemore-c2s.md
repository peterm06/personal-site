If you're starting pour-over and don't want to overthink the grinder, this is the one. The burrs are consistent enough for a clean cup, the build feels far nicer than the price suggests, and it's quick enough that grinding never becomes the reason you skip making coffee.

Pair it with a simple dripper and you have a complete, low-fuss setup. Five stars.
